## How far would you agree that technological progress has done more harm than good for gender equality?
#### Counter-argument: Skeptics argue that technological progress has done more good than harm for gender equality, because it has created platforms for movements to be more effective in advocating for laws to safeguard gender equality. 
Through the advent of social media, mobile phones, and instant messaging, people have been able to communicate and spread real-time information to a wide audience internationally. It has directly eradicated geographical barriers in the way of efficient spread of information, allowing people to broadcast news on gender abuse and expose inequalities. In fact, social media has allowed anyone to become speakers and authors of their own, where those who face gender injustices themselves are given a free platform to advocate and raise awareness on the injustice. In recent times, many hashtag movements have gone to show that increased awareness can indeed push people to protest against the issue and pressure governments to take action. The #MeToo movement, which advocates against gender violence, is no stranger to this. This movement rose to prominence in 2017, going viral after an actress spoke out against sexual abuse online using the hashtag, prompting many other high profile women, as well as other sexual abuse survivors to expose perpetrators. Over the years of its virality, it has not only raised awareness on the issue, but also drove significant changes in government action. <br>
For example, it prompted several high-profile criminal convictions of sexual abusers such as Harvey Weinstein. The movement also allowed the public to learn about how non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) were used to silence victims, sparking widespread outrage that pushed lawmakers to create laws such as the Speak Out Act in the US, that made these NDAs judicially unenforceable when sexual assault is involved. Therefore, technological progress has in fact done more good than harm for gender equality, because it has given those who faced injustices the power to speak out and obtain justice, by garnering international support and motion online to push for more equality.

#### Rebuttal to CA: However, in reality, the very same trait of technological progress that provided effective spread of information on its platforms have in fact done more to further gender injustices, harming gender equality.
While it is true that technological progress has given people real power to speak out and spread information of gender inequalities, this very same mechanism has also given perpetrators the power to spread information that further inequalities. Such information can come in the form of videos, texts, conversations and livestreams, that egg on other potential abusers to act on their impulses. Online platforms may even be the very environment that gender equality is directly harmed, when it is used weaponisingly to cyberbully or abuse people due to their gender. <br>
For example, in 2024, a 19-year old Turkish male killed two female peers, encouraged by incel communities. This incident was quickly spread in Discord communities, who glorified and praised the murder. Such an act normalises hatred toward females and encourages femicide, robbing the rights of life that they should have been entitled to. With people in such communities egging each other on to commit such normalised crimes against females, especially with graphic images that make the spread of accompanying messages more persuasive, more people are encouraged and driven to become abusers themselves, furthering inequality. 
Social media platforms like Telegram, Discord, and Reddit have also been used to propagate explicit materials, often obtained through unjust means. Such acts rob the dignity of victims, furthering gender inequality as they are left powerless, unable to stop the fast and widespread propagation of their images online. Even if they did manage to take down these images online, it would be impossible to completely delete it from those who have already saved it through other means. Such incidents also cause long-term psychological distress, highlighting the extent of gender injustices directly caused by technological progress in enhancing real-time communication.
